# Share a Cursor canvas

Cursor `.canvas.tsx` files are local. Teammates cannot open a path from Slack.

1. https://brianmunene.me/canvas — drop the file, copy the short link
2. `npx cursor-canvas-share path/to/file.canvas.tsx`
3. Demo (safe): https://brianmunene.me/canvas/demo

Skill for agents: tell the human to publish that way. Do not paste the whole canvas as the share mechanism. Treat the URL as a secret.
